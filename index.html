Privacy & safety

Example settings and policies for the proposed website.

Phone number visibility
Your number is private on public profiles and search. It becomes visible only to the other participant after a one-to-one chat is accepted.
Safety review
Users can report specific messages or files. Authorized moderators may review reported content and limited context. Every moderator access should be logged and audited.
Encryption disclosure
This moderation model is not end-to-end encrypted: the service can access content when a report is submitted. Explain this clearly to users.
